Stamp & Prompt Guide

Submit artwork or writing for a prompt to earn stamps, then spend them at the Store.

Artwork

Sketched and Colored 3
Lined and Colored 5
+ Shading +2
+ Background +3
+ Additional 11sins +50%

Writing

Every 500 words 2

Writing submissions must be 500+ words to count.

Submission Rules

  • Every prompt comes with stamps as reward.
  • For every participating 11sin, a stamp will be added to your stamp card.
  • NPCs do not count, unless you don't own an 11sin.
  • 11sins have to be in full body to count (small cutoffs are allowed).
  • You earn double stamps if you make gift art or don't own an 11sin.

Stamp Card

  • Whenever a member earns 11 stamps, they get a free stamp.
  • There is no limit to the number of stamps a member can own.
  • Stamps can be used at the Store.
