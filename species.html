About Species

Appearance: 11sins appear as anthropomorphic cat-like creatures with some strange avian qualities to them. One of the most noticeable being the floating hands with small bird bodies. 11sins have cape-like wings. They lack arms, nose and eyes but their entire face is taken over by a big grinning mouth. Size vary per 11sins, but the average 11sins is that of a greater Rhea (approximately 14 burgers tall). They come in variety of colors, pattern and unique designs.

Lifestyle: 11sins can be found everywhere, they adapt to their surroundings and make the best out of it. Although they don’t stay long at one place but once they have found their ideal spot they settle for around 11 years until their flying instincts kicks in and take off for another journey. They like to be in the open sky and fly to new places. 11sins are very social creatures and get along wonderfully with foreign creatures of all kind.

Diet: They are omnivores. 11sins can’t cook, all they know is McDonald’s, charge their phones, twerk, be bisexual, eat hot chips and die.
