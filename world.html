About World

It started with one, the God of Sin. It's very nature was an error in the divine order that grew too large to contain. Other gods saw in it's existence only a threat that had to be erradicated.

The God of sins fought back and for some time it held its own. But as it's strength began to weaken and it's end closed near, in a final desperate choice, it shattered itself.

The explosion of its existence sent eleven pieces and thousand of shards across the world. Where they landed they took root and became the 11 Sins.

Of those eleven fragments, Four were of pure destruction, the Horsemen of the Apocalypse. The other seven fragments manifested as seven Deadly sins, each carrying a piece of that original gods power.

The shards spread in every corner of the world, wielding less power that the fragments, but still being able to create life, the followers of the Sin, the 11Sins.
