About World

It started with one: the God of Sin. Its very nature was an error in the divine order that grew too large to contain. Other gods saw its existence as nothing more than a threat that had to be eradicated.

The God of Sins fought back, and for some time, it held its own. But as its strength began to weaken and its end drew near, it made one final, desperate choice: it shattered itself.

The explosion of its existence sent eleven pieces and thousands of shards across the world. Where they landed, they took root and became the Eleven Sins.

Of those eleven fragments, four embodied pure destruction: the Horsemen of the Apocalypse. The other seven manifested as the Seven Deadly Sins, each carrying a piece of the original god's power.

The shards spread to every corner of the world. Though they wielded far less power than the fragments themselves, they still possessed the ability to create life: the followers of Sin, known as the 11Sins.
